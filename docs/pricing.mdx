## What's Included

Every subscription includes full access to all PERSCOM features:

- **Personnel Management** — Track and manage your entire organization
- **Records** — Assignment, award, rank, service, combat, qualification, and training records
- **Forms** — Build and deploy custom forms
- **Calendar & Events** — Schedule and manage organizational events
- **API Access** — Full access to the PERSCOM API
- **Webhooks** — Real-time notifications for external integrations
- **Automations** — Trigger actions automatically based on events
- **Widgets** — Embed personnel data on your website
- **Single Sign-On** — OAuth 2.0 and social login support
- **Custom Domain** — Use your own subdomain
- **Data Export** — Export and backup your data
- **Support** — Email and ticket support
