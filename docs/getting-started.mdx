## What To Expect

During your trial, you have full access to the platform to explore its features and evaluate how PERSCOM fits your organization's needs.

After the 7-day trial period ends, subscribe to a plan to continue using PERSCOM. Visit the [Pricing](/docs/pricing) page to compare subscription options.

## Next Steps

1. **Create your account** at [perscom.io/register](https://perscom.io/register)
2. **Set up your organization** with basic information
3. **Add personnel** to start managing your team
4. **Explore features** like records, forms, and calendars
5. **Configure integrations** if you need to connect external services
